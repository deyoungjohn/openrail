# Openrail

Get paid in stablecoins: send an invoice link, your client pays in what they hold on any of 8 chains, and every payment is matched to its invoice and lands in your account in about 30 seconds, with a receipt anyone can verify. Built on NEAR (NEAR Intents, Chain Signatures).

Designed and tested before 1 Nov; see `evidence/`.

Work in progress for the NEAR Hackathon (1–14 Nov 2026). This README is regenerated from `docs/src` as the product is built.
